## Take a Cushion

Take a cushion extends the functionality of the vanilla cushion to allow the ability for other mobs to ride it. A great addition to any vanilla+ packs!

![Villager on a cushion](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b547ca6b22e4b7306d2e156fcfc9d49492747472.png)

When placing a cushion all you have to do is sneak with a saddle in your off-hand.
The cushion will now work like a boat and will pick up a nearby mob that gets close enough to it.
